01 About the channel

Technology, explained as if you were sitting beside us.

Abdullah IT Center started with one honest frustration: most IT tutorials assume you already know half of what they're saying. That's not how learning works. Here, every lesson begins from zero, moves at a human pace, and shows the whole process — including the mistakes.

Whether you're a student preparing for exams, someone switching careers, or simply trying to get comfortable with the computer you already own — this channel is built for you. The goal isn't to make you impressive. It's to make you capable.

  • i.Beginner-friendly lessons with zero jargon and no assumed knowledge.
  • ii.Real, practical examples you can follow along with on your own machine.
  • iii.Helpful guidance on digital tools, everyday tech and earning skills.
  • iv.New tutorials every week — always free, always ad-light, always yours.
